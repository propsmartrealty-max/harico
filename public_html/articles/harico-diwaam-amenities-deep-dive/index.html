Harico Diwaam Amenities Deep Dive: Sky Deck, Creator Studio, 12 Guest Suites & 100+ Lifestyle Features

Explore every amenity at Harico Diwaam Kiwale — from the 24th-floor Sky Observation Deck to Dolby Atmos cinema, Wellness Spa, and India's first Creator Studio inside a residential complex.

⚡ Key Executive Takeaways

  • 24th-floor Sky Observation Deck with glass-floor lookout and Sahyadri mountain panoramas.
  • India's first residential Creator Studio — soundproof podcast booth + YouTube production studio + 1 Gbps fiber.
  • 12 fully furnished luxury guest suites for visiting family — bookable for up to 7 nights.
  • 40-seater Dolby Atmos mini-theatre with 4K laser projection and reclining cinema seating.
  • Infinity pool, wellness spa with jacuzzi & sauna, Planet Kids Zone, and rooftop barbecue party deck.
Harico Diwaam Kiwale offers 100+ curated resort and lifestyle amenities across 6 themed zones — Sky, Wellness, Entertainment, Sports, Kids, and Business. From a 24th-floor glass-floor Sky Deck to India's first residential Creator Studio.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the Sky Observation Deck at Harico Diwaam?

The 24th-Floor Sky Observation Deck features a glass-floor lookout panel, high-powered telescopes, a sunset meditation pavilion, and social seating for 60+ residents with 360° views of the Sahyadri mountains and Sentosa Water Park.

Does Harico Diwaam have a swimming pool?

Yes, Harico Diwaam features a 25-metre resort-style infinity pool with panoramic hill views, a separate children's wading area, pool cabanas, and heated water during winter.

What is the Creator Studio at Harico Diwaam?

The Creator Studio is a professionally equipped media production facility including a soundproof podcast booth, YouTube/Reels studio with green screen and ring lights, and 1-Gbps symmetric fiber — the first such facility in any residential project in Kiwale.

How many guest suites does Harico Diwaam have?

Harico Diwaam has 12 fully furnished, air-conditioned luxury guest suites available for booking by residents for visiting family and friends for up to 7 consecutive nights at no additional cost.

Is there a mini theatre at Harico Diwaam?

Yes — the 40-seater Dolby Atmos mini-theatre features stadium-terraced reclining luxury cinema seats, a 4K laser projector, and Dolby Atmos 7.1.4 surround sound. Bookable for private screenings and events.

How many amenities does Harico Diwaam Kiwale have?

Harico Diwaam offers 100+ curated lifestyle and resort amenities across 6 themed zones: Sky Zone, Wellness Zone, Entertainment Zone, Sports Zone, Kids Zone, and Business Zone.
